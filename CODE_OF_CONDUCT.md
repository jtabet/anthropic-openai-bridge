# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming bad.
- Disagree about technical decisions, not about people.
- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language are not tolerated.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates the spirit of this document, email **dev@jeremietabet.com**. Reports will be handled confidentially and in good faith.
